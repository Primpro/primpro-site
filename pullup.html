Pull Up

Show up as you. Pull up ready.

Pull Up helps young people find their confidence, build their personal brand and step into work and opportunity. It ends with a dressed up showcase where they meet people from the industries they want to join.

The programme

Four days. One glow up.

DAY 1

Pull up as you

Identity and confidence. Being yourself, owning your story and what it really takes to make it as a creative.

DAY 2

Pull up online

Personal branding. Social media that works for you, your CV and LinkedIn, plus a content shoot so everyone leaves with new photos and video.

DAY 3

Pull up ready

Applying yourself in the real world. Jobs, side hustles, interviews, money basics and spotting scams.

DAY 4

The Pull Up

Everyone dressed up. A showcase, networking with guests from work and creative industries, and a proper celebration.

"I've never seen them that locked in for a full hour."
Where it started

It started with one talk at Croydon College.

Adedolapo Kazeem, Michael Okunloro and a friend went in to talk to students about being yourself, being creative and finding your way. A staff member told them they had never seen students that engaged for a whole hour.

Pull Up turns that talk into a full programme, built by people who came up the same way. Immigrant journeys, creative careers, real jobs and real hustle.
