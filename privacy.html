This policy explains what personal information we collect through primpro.co.uk, why we collect it, and your rights over it.

This website is run by Primpro Ltd, trading as The P Collective. We are the data controller for your information.

For anything about your data, email hello@primpro.co.uk.

Cookies and tracking

We use Google Analytics, the Meta Pixel and the TikTok Pixel to understand visits and measure adverts. None of these load unless you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you click Decline, they stay off.

We store your choice in your browser so we don't ask on every visit. You can change it any time using the Cookie settings link.

Young people and Pull Up

Pull Up works with young people, some of whom are under 18. This website is for colleges, schools, partners and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from under 18s through this website. Information about workshop participants is handled separately with the college or school, under their own safeguarding and data arrangements, and with consent from a parent or guardian where required.

Who we share it with

Only with services that help us run the business, and only what they need:

  • Google Workspace, for email
  • Vercel, which hosts this website
  • Google, Meta and TikTok, for analytics and adverts, only if you accept cookies
  • Couriers, payment providers and our accountant, when you buy or book something
  • Artists we represent, when you buy or commission their work, so they know who owns it

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't go anywhere: up to 2 years. Sales and booking records: 6 years, as UK tax law requires. Cookie choices: until you clear them or change them.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to someone else. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email hello@primpro.co.uk and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
