MCCXLV

Ogedengbe Oladimeji
Said /Maekli:v/
Conceptual abstract artistNigeriaSelf taught
"From ashes, we mould. I explore the beauty within imperfection."

Ogedengbe Oladimeji, known as MCCXLV, is a Nigerian conceptual abstract artist whose work explores the complexity of beauty within imperfection. He creates layered psychological environments shaped by memory, tension and lived experience, translating fragments of existence into carefully constructed compositions.

Entirely self taught, his visual language has grown through experimentation and repetition. A background in photography sharpened his eye for framing, light and spatial balance. He often says that if he were not painting or working behind the camera, he would be cooking. To him, all three are acts of composition, timing and care.

His work has been shown at Uber Live, the ICAF Art Show, Social Media Week, Art Summit Nigeria and the first Street Souk in Lagos, and has sold to a private collector in New York. He has also led creative direction and photography for artists including Mohbad, Ajebutter and Naira Marley's UK tour merchandise.

"I paint because the work deserves to exist, and I intend for it to be encountered in museums, galleries, airports, and other unexpected spaces."
